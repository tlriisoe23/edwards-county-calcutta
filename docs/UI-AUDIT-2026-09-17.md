## 2. What the research says (patterns worth borrowing, and what not to copy)

Full report: `ui-audit-2026-09-17-evidence/research.md`. The recommendations below are the ones that change what these two apps should do.

1. **A 2–3 field fast path for the most frequent action.** Auctria "Quick Entry", ClickBid "Butler" and Settle Up Golf all converge on *identify the line + amount + one button* for recording a sale. Calcutta already has this shape; the leaderboard's score sheet should be measured against it (how many keystrokes from "card arrives" to "saved").
2. **Distinct Sold / Pass / Void actions, and a first-class undo.** StreamBid's clerk console separates them; Auctria explicitly lacks undo. Calcutta has all three plus Undo; the leaderboard's equivalents (Remove, Restore, Lock/Unlock) are inconsistent about confirmation and reversibility.
3. **The TV is its own presentation surface with its own few settings** (Golf Genius, BidParTee, BlueGolf): font size, page order, dwell, dark/glare mode. Both apps do this; the leaderboard over-does it (three "after this many pages" numbers, px labels).
4. **Show only what matters right now on live surfaces; push setup off-screen** (Golf Genius "Top X", Golf Pad's events-vs-live split). This is the single biggest gap in both operator consoles: setup, sharing, theme and history controls sit beside the live task at equal weight.
5. **Distance-based type sizing**: Daktronics' verified rule is roughly 1 inch of character height per 25 ft of viewing distance (a stricter 1 in/10 ft is also common). Measure the clubhouse TV's viewing distance once and set the default TV text size from it, instead of a 24–64 px field.
6. **Info slides: 7–10 s, "3 lines × 5 words"**; return to live content promptly.
7. **Context next to the current item** (Calcuttapp shows handicap/recent scores by the current lot; Calcutta League shows a countdown). Calcutta's block card already shows the flight and members; the leaderboard's public and TV leader strip could carry "thru" and "today" the same way.
8. **Do not copy** the gala suites' breadth (ticketing, CRM, text-to-give). The right structural comparables are the narrow golf tools. Neither app has this problem today; the risk is accretion (the Leaderboard has gained Quick entry, statistics, a Calcutta board, themes and auto-flighting in a week).

### 4.1 Operator console shell

**UI-CA-01 · Operator (compact) · P2 · Source-supported (SR-CAL-01) · main + UI3** — **Compact mode hides the connection state, the event name and the Demo tag — and compact turns itself on exactly while the auction is LIVE.** `refinements.css:57` hides `.admin-heading` and `.event-toolbar` when `.compact.console-active`; those two elements hold the only operator connection indicator (`operator.tsx:273`), the only place the event name and its " · Demo" suffix appear, and the operator identity. Compact is automatic while LIVE/PAUSED (`:228`). The console toolbar shows `e.calcuttaName` **only when no team is on the block** (`:275`). So during the one hour that matters the clerk cannot see that the server went away, cannot see which event they are in, and cannot see that they are hammering sales into the demonstration event. The masthead reads "THE CALCUTTA / EDWARDS COUNTY" for every event. *Recommendation:* move the connection pill and a compact event line ("Spring Calcutta 2026 · Demo") into `console-toolbar` beside the status badge; keep the pool line; make Demo a warning-toned pill that never hides. *Accept:* with compact on and a team on the block, the viewport shows event name, Demo tag (if demo) and connection state without scrolling; killing the server flips the pill to "Reconnecting…" within one poll.

**UI-CA-02 · Operator · P2 · Source-supported (SR-CAL-02) · UI3 regression** — **The console's own "Full screen" button hides the masthead, and with it Undo.** `globals.css:21` (`:fullscreen .console-active>.mast{display:none}`) predates UI3; UI3-3 moved Undo, Compact view and the theme picker *into* `.mast` (`operator.tsx:255-260, 273`). The console toolbar still offers `Full screen` (`:275`). Pressing it reintroduces the exact gap Batch L §3 was written to close — "Undo last action was unreachable during a LIVE auction" — leaving only the `U` key. The button's own tooltip has to explain that it "does not open the TV display", which is a sign it is a decoy: since Batch K the TV is a separate popup and the console has no reason to be fullscreen. *Recommendation:* delete the `:fullscreen … > .mast` rule (the compact masthead is already 54 px), or remove `Full screen` from the console entirely. *Accept:* in fullscreen console the Undo button is visible and clickable; no tooltip is needed to tell Full screen apart from Launch TV Display, because only one exists.

**UI-CA-03 · Operator · P2 · Source-supported (SR-CAL-13) · main + UI3, worse in compact** — **Seven sections have no tab, and the headings are slogans instead of names.** `TabsContent` values `teams`, `buyers`, `rules`, `sharing`, `help`, `activity`, `access` have no `TabsTrigger` in `mainTabs` (`operator.tsx:245-251`), so on any of them the strip shows nothing selected. The headings are "Your field, ready to go.", "Every hammer, on record.", "Settle up, with confidence.", "One link for your spectators.", "The audit trail". A clerk who follows Tools ▾ › Activity lands on a tab bar with nothing lit and a heading that does not say "Activity"; in compact mode with Setup steps collapsed, the word "Teams" appears nowhere on the Teams tab. *Recommendation:* headings name the section in the same words as the entry point ("Teams & flights", "Sales", "Settlement", "Display & sharing") — the slogans can become the eyebrow; when `tab` is not one of the five strip values, render a crumb at the left of `op-nav-bar` ("Tools › Activity"). *Accept:* every operator section has an on-screen `h2` equal to its entry label; the tab bar always shows either a selected tab or a crumb.

**UI-CA-04 · Operator · P3 · Source-supported (SR-CAL-16, SR-CAL-26) · UI3 placement; duplication on main** — **Eight equal-weight controls in a 54 px masthead, and the theme is editable in two places with two different save models.** `operator.tsx:262-273` puts Public board, Launch TV Display, Tools ▾, Sign out, Undo, an event-theme `Choice` (visible text is just "Classic ECGC" — no visible label, `aria-label` only), Compact view and a conditional `Auto` in one `<nav>` at the same 14 px weight. The same theme is editable in Display & sharing, where it needs an explicit `Save theme` (`theme-settings.tsx:28`), while the masthead select saves on change (`:257`). Separately, `Brand` is a link (`auction.tsx:15`) and the operator uses it at `:262`, so clicking the logo mid-auction navigates the console tab to the public board. *For the owner to weigh:* the pin was requested in UI3-3. If it stays, give the select a visible "Theme" prefix; otherwise move it into Tools ▾ and let Display & sharing be the one place with descriptions. Keep Undo and Compact pinned — they are the two that matter at 9 pm. Render `Brand` as a non-link in the operator. *Accept:* masthead holds ≤ 5 controls at 1366 px; the theme is changeable from one place plus at most one shortcut, both saving the same way; no same-tab navigation away from `/admin` from the masthead.

**UI-CA-05 · Operator · P3 · Source-supported (SR-CAL-14, SR-CAL-15, SR-CAL-25) · main + UI3** — **The five prepare cards head every tab in every phase, carry wrong readiness states, and the empty desk has no buttons.** `operator.tsx:274` always renders `{prepareBlock}` above the tab bar in the non-compact branch; `phase` (`:225`) is computed but only changes a tag. After the auction (`COMPLETED` → `autoCompact` false) the five 100 px cards return to the top of Results, Settlement and Exports — Batch K asked for them to collapse "during active operation", and afterwards they are still dead weight. Step 3 shows "Not started" on a step whose own help says "Optional" (`:224`, `:241`); steps 1, 4 and 5 carry no readiness at all; step 5 stays a plain "Start Auction" card while the auction is already live. The empty desk (`:274`) says "Create an event … or load the demonstration event" with no buttons — `New event` is in the toolbar above and `Load demo` moved inside Tools ▾ in UI3-3. *Recommendation:* render the prepare block through one `Collapsible` in every mode, open by default only while `phase === 'prepare'`; step 3 → grey "Optional" until a buyer exists; step 5 → "Auction is live" / "Completed" and disabled; put `New event` and `Load demo` inside the empty state. *Accept:* on Settlement after COMPLETED the tab strip is the first thing under the masthead; while LIVE the step 5 card is not clickable; a first-time owner reaches both starting actions without opening a menu.

### 4.2 Live auction console

This is the screen the product exists for. It is also the best screen in either product: in compact mode the bid field sits at y=559 and the hammer is fully above the 900 px fold. The findings below are about the last few seconds of each lot, not the layout.

**UI-CA-06 · Console · P2 · Source-supported (SR-CAL-03) · UI3-only** — **The Undo confirmation calls a sale "sell".** `lib/audit.ts:12-22` maps `sale`, `finish`, `settlement_payment`, `settlement_disbursement` — but the server writes the *request action name* into `audit.action` (`route.ts:507`), and the client sends `act('sell')`, `act('results')`, `act('settlement_record')`, `act('sale_edit')`, `act('team_import')`, `act('payout_save')`, `local_user_*`. None of those keys exist in `actionNames`, so `describeUndo` falls back to `entry.action.replaceAll('_',' ')` (`lib/audit.ts:46`). After a mistaken hammer the dialog reads **"This will undo: sell — Reed / Foster · $450, recorded 8:12 PM by op@test."** The Undo button's hover tip shows the same raw word all evening. `tests/ui3-reorder.mjs:66` asserts against a synthetic `action: 'sale'` row, so the test passes while production never emits `sale` — the guard is testing a value that does not occur. *Recommendation:* key `actionNames` by the real action names, and add a test that enumerates the `case "…"` labels in `route.ts` and fails on any missing key. *Accept:* after a sale the Undo tip reads "This will undo: Sale recorded — Reed / Foster · $450 …"; the new test enumerates every server action.

**UI-CA-07 · Console · P2 · Source-supported (SR-CAL-04) · UI3 (button) + main (`U`)** — **Undo is enabled when there is nothing to undo, and the same action explains itself two different ways.** `operator.tsx:256` sets `disabled={busy || offline}` without checking `lastUndoable(meta.audit)`; clicking opens a dialog whose description says "There is no recorded action to undo yet." above a live `Confirm` button that posts `undo` and earns a server error toast (`route.ts:464`). The two callers pass different follow-on sentences for one action: the `U` key says "The correction remains in the audit trail." (`:206`), the button says "Other operator changes are protected by a version check." (`:256`) — the second is jargon aimed at a volunteer. *Recommendation:* `disabled={… || !lastUndoable(meta.audit)}` with a tip saying why; one sentence for both paths; label the action button "Undo". *Accept:* on a fresh event the Undo button is disabled with an explanatory tip; `U` and the button open identical dialogs.

**UI-CA-08 · Console · P2 · Source-supported (SR-CAL-07) · main + UI3** — **The bid field appends instead of replacing, so the documented keyboard path can record a wrong number.** `operator.tsx:121` keeps the input equal to the server bid; `:197` focuses it on `B`; the `Input` at `:275` has no `onFocus` select. A clerk who presses `B` and types "1300" over a "1250" field sends "12501300" — rejected if it misses the increment rule, **accepted as a $12,501,300 bid if it does not**. *Recommendation:* `onFocus={ev => ev.target.select()}` plus `select()` after `focus()` in the `B` handler; set `min={s.minBid/100}` instead of `min="0"`. *Accept:* `B`, type `1300`, Enter records $1,300.

**UI-CA-09 · Console · P2 · Source-supported (SR-CAL-08) · main + UI3** — **The sale cannot be confirmed from the keyboard, and focus is lost afterwards.** The hammer in the Sold dialog (`auction-controls.tsx:21`) is not a submit and nothing handles Enter at dialog level. When the dialog is opened by `S` (`operator.tsx:198-200`) `document.activeElement` is `body`, so Radix returns focus to `body` on close and nothing refocuses the bid field. Same for the Undo dialog via `U`. The keyboard path the Help screen advertises therefore stops halfway and hands the clerk back to the mouse between every lot. *Recommendation:* wrap the dialog body in a `<form onSubmit>` so Enter confirms once a buyer is chosen (guarding while the combobox list is open, reusing `escapeClosesListFirst`'s pattern); add `onCloseAutoFocus={() => bidInput.current?.focus()}` to both dialogs. *Accept:* `S` → `↓` `Enter` → `Enter` records the sale; after close, focus is in the bid field with the next team on the block.

**UI-CA-10 · Console · P2 · Source-supported (SR-CAL-09) · main + UI3** — **When nothing is on the block the console offers no way forward except a 32 px button in a list.** With auto-advance off, after `Mark unsold` / `Skip for now` on the last-but-one team, or after undoing a `block` action (`route.ts:462+` resets `teamId: null`), `current` is undefined: the Block reads "The block is open.", every bid control and the Hammer are disabled (`:275`), and the only affordance is the small `On block` button in each queue row. The room is waiting while the clerk hunts. *Recommendation:* when `e.status === 'LIVE' && !current && next.length`, replace the disabled Hammer with a primary "Put Reed / Foster on the block" and give it the `S` key. *Accept:* with auto-advance off, a sale → one click or keystroke → the next team is on the block.

**UI-CA-11 · Console · P3 · Source-supported (SR-CAL-17) · main + UI3** — Up to eight *Next up* rows each render ↑, ↓, `On block` and `Skip for now` — up to 32 buttons in the aside, with `On block` no more prominent than a reorder arrow. The empty-queue message says "Return unsold teams from the Teams tab." while compact mode has hidden Teams behind Setup steps. *Recommendation:* `On block` as the row's only visible button, ↑/↓/Skip behind a `…` menu (the roster already has that pattern) or on hover/focus-within; make the empty-state sentence a `notice-link` that opens the tab. *Accept:* ≤ 2 visible controls per queue row; the empty-queue message is a link.

**UI-CA-12 · Console · P3 · Source-supported (SR-CAL-18) · main + UI3** — `Correct bid` is a second bid button differing from `Record bid` by one flag (`correction:true`), sitting in the increments row; `Record bid` is disabled when not LIVE and `Correct bid` is not. *Recommendation:* one `Record bid`; when the amount is below the current bid or increment, route through `ask('Lower the bid to $X?', …)` with the same disabled conditions. *Accept:* the increments row contains only amounts; a lower amount asks to confirm the correction.

**UI-CA-13 · Console · P3 · Source-supported (SR-CAL-38) · main + UI3** — Offline disables `Record bid`, the quick amounts, `Hammer / sold` and `Undo`, but not `Pause`, `Complete`, `Mark unsold`, `Skip for now`, `On block`, `Correct bid` or any table action — those toast instead. *Recommendation:* one `disabledWhileOffline = busy || offline` applied uniformly, with the connection pill from UI-CA-01 saying why. *Accept:* with the server down, every mutating control on the console is disabled and the pill explains it.

**UI-CA-14 · Console · P3 · Hypothesis (SR-CAL-37)** — **The clerk cannot see what the room can see.** The console's Block always shows bid and bidder (`auction.tsx:21` `(admin || s.showBid)`); the public board respects `showBid` / `showBuyer` / `showSalePrice` (`lib/model.ts:12`). Nothing on the console states which are on. *Named check:* turn `showBid` off in Advanced › Public visibility, run the console, and watch whether a clerk narrating "as you can see on the board…" is wrong. *Recommendation:* a muted "Room sees: bid · buyer · pools" line in the toolbar. *Accept:* toggling a public flag changes that line within one poll.

### 4.3 Confirmations and lifecycle

**UI-CA-15 · Operator (all dialogs) · P2 · Source-supported (SR-CAL-05) · main + UI3** — **Every destructive confirmation's action button reads "Confirm".** `operator.tsx:304` renders `confirm?.label || 'Confirm'` and only `skip` passes a label. "Void this sale?", "Delete team?", "Withdraw team?", "Complete this auction?", "Revoke operator access?", "Reset demo data?" and "Change the team on the block?" all end in `[Cancel] [Confirm]` in the default primary style, so the dialog never restates what is about to happen and never looks dangerous. *Recommendation:* every `ask()` passes a verb label ("Void sale", "Delete team", "Complete auction", "Undo") and a `destructive` flag switching `AlertDialogAction` to `variant="destructive"` — `globals.css:186` already themes it. *Accept:* no confirmation dialog's action button reads "Confirm".

**UI-CA-16 · Console / Prepare 5 · P2 · Source-supported (SR-CAL-06) · main (Restart) + UI3 (step 5)** — **Restarting a completed auction takes one unconfirmed click.** `operator.tsx:275` renders one button that reads Resume / Restart / Start by status; from COMPLETED a single click with no `ask()` flips the public board from "Final auction summary" back to live bidding and re-enables the hammer, while `Complete` — the less consequential direction — does confirm. Only the ControlTip explains. In UI3 the step 5 card is rendered identically while LIVE, PAUSED or COMPLETED (`:243` `current: false` always); choosing an option calls `act('status', {status:'LIVE'})` again, re-opening the TV popup or, on the "here" path (`:187-188`), navigating the console window itself to `/tv` mid-auction. *Recommendation:* route Restart through `ask('Reopen the completed auction?', 'The public board returns to live bidding. Recorded sales are kept.')`; make step 5 render "Auction is live" / "Completed — restart from the console" and never call `startAuction` when status ≠ SETUP/READY. *Accept:* from COMPLETED, restarting always confirms; while LIVE the step 5 card is inert.

### 4.4 Prepare — Event & rules

**UI-CA-17 · Rules · P2 · Source-supported (SR-CAL-11) · main + UI3** — **A payout ladder is rendered for every flight *and* the combined pool regardless of pool mode.** `rules.tsx:38` maps every flight plus a hard-coded `combined` entry into a full `PayoutLadder`, whatever `s.poolMode` is, although `lib/model.ts:15-23` already knows the real pools. With three flights in "One combined pool" the clerk edits four ladders, three of which do nothing; in "Separate" mode the "Combined / shared pool" ladder is a phantom whose own fine print admits "This ladder is saved for when this pool is used." *Recommendation:* render ladders for the pools `compute()` reports, with a one-line note naming the mode that decided it. *Accept:* the number of ladders equals the number of live pools; changing pool mode changes which ladders appear.

**UI-CA-18 · Rules / Results · P2 · Source-supported (SR-CAL-10) · main + UI3** — **Unsaved rule and results drafts are discarded silently on any tab switch.** `rules.tsx:14/16` and the Results positions (`:40`) are component state, and `operator.tsx:286-287` mounts them inside `TabsContent` without `forceMount`, so Radix unmounts the inactive panel. Clicking any tab, prepare card or Tools item throws the draft away with no prompt; there is no dirty indicator on `Save event & rules` and no `beforeunload`. *Recommendation:* lift the draft to the parent, or `forceMount` the panels, plus a dirty marker and a confirm on navigation away. *Accept:* editing a rule, switching tabs and returning preserves the edit, or the switch asks first.

**UI-CA-19 · Rules · P3 · Source-supported (SR-CAL-36, SR-CAL-22) · main + UI3** — The only place the READY status exists is an aside card three sections deep in Rules ("Event status · Current: SETUP · Mark ready · Return to setup"); no prepare step mentions it, the public board shows "READY TO BEGIN" for it, and step 5 goes straight SETUP→LIVE. `Return to setup` — the only way to un-LIVE without completing — lives there too. Separately, `operator.tsx:275` hard-codes `$` in the bid field while `Currency` offers USD/CAD/GBP/EUR/AUD, `AmountEditor` prefixes the ISO code ("USD"), Settlement labels "Amount (USD)" and Rules says "(currency units)". *Recommendation:* either drop READY or make it step 5's first press; move `Return to setup` beside Pause in the console toolbar under a confirm; add one `currencySymbol(currency)` helper used by all four sites. *Accept:* status changes are all reachable from the console toolbar or step 5; a GBP event shows £ in bid entry, amount editors and settlement.

### 4.5 Prepare — Teams, buyers, display

**UI-CA-20 · Teams · P2 · Source-supported (SR-CAL-12) · main + UI3** — **Two adjacent buttons both say "CSV", the primary add button does not say "Add", and three lower-case buttons rewrite the auction order with one unconfirmed click.** `operator.tsx:276` places `CSV` (download) next to `Bulk paste / CSV` (upload); the primary is `Full team editor`; the quick-add submit says `Add another` before any team exists. `:279` renders `['name','flight','handicap']` as raw lower-case button labels, and one click calls `act('team_reorder')`, which **renumbers the lots for the whole field** with no confirmation and a `quiet` toast — while the column it sorts by is titled "Index" and the button says "handicap". *Recommendation:* "Export CSV" / "Import teams…" / "Add team" / quick-add "Add team"; replace the three sort buttons with one `Choice` "Reorder by… Name / Flight / Index" routed through `ask('Reorder the auction by name? Lots will be renumbered.')` and a non-quiet toast. *Accept:* no two buttons on the tab share the word "CSV"; a reorder always confirms and names its effect.

**UI-CA-21 · Teams · P3 · Source-supported (SR-CAL-24) · main + UI3** — The row menu renders Edit, Duplicate, Put on block, Return to queue, Mark unsold, Withdraw and Delete for every row regardless of state: "Return to queue" on an UPCOMING team is a no-op, and "Mark unsold" on a SOLD team is refused server-side *after* the clerk confirms. *Recommendation:* show items by `t.status`, with Delete last and destructively styled. *Accept:* an UPCOMING row's menu has no "Return to queue".

**UI-CA-22 · Buyers / Display & sharing · P3 · Source-supported (SR-CAL-15, SR-CAL-21) · main + UI3** — Buyers is a well-made optional screen whose prepare card still says "Not started"; its cards use `h2` under the tab `h2`. Display & sharing is entered from a card labelled "TV / Display Settings" (Title Case, unlike every other prepare label) but is headed "Display & sharing", and after UI3 the TV launcher exists in four places — masthead, step 5 dialog, this screen and Help. *Recommendation:* card label = destination heading; buyer cards `h3`; keep "Open TV display" here and in step 5 and make Help link to step 5. *Accept:* each prepare card's label equals its destination's `h2`; one TV launcher per screen.

### 4.6 After the auction — Sales, Results, Settlement

**UI-CA-23 · Sales · P3 · Source-supported (SR-CAL-23) · main + UI3** — On a default event (`buybackMode: 'off'`) the Ownership column renders "Purchaser 100%" on every row — a column that always says the same thing, using a third word for "buyer". Statuses render as raw enums with no tone: `ACTIVE` / `VOID` / `REOPENED` in Sales, `ON_BLOCK` under each Results team, `["UPCOMING","ON_BLOCK",…]` in the public filter. The Correct-sale dialog uses a plain `Choice` of all buyers while the Sold dialog uses a searchable combobox with recent chips, so the correction path is the slow one at 40 buyers. *Recommendation:* hide the ownership column when buybacks are off; one shared `statusLabel()` in `lib/model.ts` with tone classes, used in all three places; reuse `BuyerPicker` in the correction dialog. *Accept:* a default event's Sales table has five columns; no on-screen string contains an underscore or an all-caps enum other than the LIVE/PAUSED badge.

**UI-CA-24 · Results · P3 · Source-supported (SR-CAL-35) · main + UI3** — The place inputs are enabled at any status while `Save final positions` is disabled unless COMPLETED, and the notice above explains rather than prevents — so a treasurer can type twelve finishing places and only then discover Save is dead. *Recommendation:* enable the inputs and Save together, with the notice carrying a "Complete the auction" link to the console — **or** allow saving before completion, which is a business decision and should not be assumed. *Accept:* the inputs and Save are enabled and disabled together.

**UI-CA-25 · Settlement · P3 · Source-supported (SR-CAL-34) · main + UI3** — **The accountant's words are the headline and the treasurer's words are the footnote.** `settlement.tsx:20` makes `AUCTION RECEIVABLES` / `TOURNAMENT PAYABLES` the 12 px letter-spaced eyebrow above the big number, with "Remaining to collect" / "Remaining to pay" subordinate beneath. `Mark paid` records `account.balance` but the button does not say how much it is about to record. *Recommendation:* swap the roles ("Still to collect" as the label, "receivables" nowhere on screen); `Mark paid · $1,250`; `Record partial payment` → `Record a payment…`, defaulting the dialog amount to the balance (it already has `max={balance}`), which also merges two buttons per card into one. *Accept:* no on-screen "receivable", "payable" or "disbursement"; the full-balance button shows the amount.

Help › "Before guests arrive" still describes the pre-step-5 sequence ("Open the TV display and choose Full screen…"), which step 5 now does and asks about. *Accept:* the dialog shows `new URL('/admin', window.location.origin)`; the checklist mentions step 5.

## 5. Cross-cutting — what the two products share

These are patterns present in **both** products, where the same decision (or the same fix) applies to each. IDs are `UI-X-nn`. Where one product has already solved something the other has not, that is said explicitly: the fastest route to a better UI here is usually to copy the sibling rather than invent.

**UI-X-01 · P2 · Both · Observed** — **Setup sits at the same visual weight as the live task, in both consoles.** This is the single biggest structural gap the research points at (§2.4: Golf Genius "Top X", Golf Pad's events-vs-live split). In the Leaderboard the score sheet — the desk's entire purpose — begins at y=903 on a 1440×900 screen and y=1413 on a phone, under a masthead, an eyebrow, an H1 with a theme select and a "+ New tournament" primary, two status indicators, an event selector row, a sticky banner, a tab bar, a two-row toolbar and a 50-word paragraph (UI-LB-01). In Calcutta the five 100 px prepare cards head every tab in every phase, including after the auction is over (UI-CA-05). **Calcutta has already solved the live case and the Leaderboard has not:** compact mode puts the bid field at y=559 and the hammer entirely above the fold. *Recommendation:* the Leaderboard adopts Calcutta's state-driven compaction — when an event is In Progress, setup controls collapse behind one disclosure and the sheet comes to the top; Calcutta extends its existing `Collapsible` to the non-compact phases. *Accept:* in each app, the primary work surface of the current phase starts within 600 px of the top at 1440×900.

**UI-X-02 · P2 · Both · Source-supported** — **Reversibility is inconsistent, and each product is strong exactly where the other is weak.** Calcutta has a first-class Undo with an audit trail, and confirms nearly everything — but every confirmation's button reads "Confirm" in the default primary style, so the dialog neither restates the act nor looks dangerous (UI-CA-15), and Undo is enabled when there is nothing to undo (UI-CA-07). The Leaderboard has no Undo at all, and its equivalents disagree with each other: Remove, Clear and Restore confirm, while **Lock final positions — which pins every public position — fires with no confirmation** (UI-LB-09). The research (§2.2) found this is exactly where comparable tools fail: StreamBid separates Sold/Pass/Void, Auctria explicitly lacks undo. *Recommendation:* the Leaderboard borrows Calcutta's audit-backed undo for Remove/Lock/Import-apply; Calcutta gives every `ask()` a verb label and a destructive flag. *Accept:* in both apps, no destructive action is unconfirmed and no confirmation button reads "Confirm"; every destructive action is either reversible or says that it is not.

**UI-X-03 · P2 · Both · Source-supported** — **One setting, two homes, two different save models — in both products.** The Leaderboard's theme is a `<select>` in the page header that saves to the server on change with no confirmation, and has no home in Event setup at all (UI-LB-02); its publishing state is split between a status `<select>` saved with the whole Event-setup form and a "Make active leaderboard" button in the selector row (UI-LB-03). Calcutta's theme is a masthead `Choice` that saves on change *and* a radio group in Display & sharing that needs an explicit `Save theme` (UI-CA-04). A volunteer who learns one of the two models is wrong half the time. *Recommendation:* one home per setting, plus at most one shortcut that behaves identically; a setting that writes to the server on change must say so. *Accept:* in each app, every setting is editable from exactly one screen, and any shortcut to it saves the same way.

**UI-X-04 · P2 · Both · Source-supported** — **Internals reach the volunteer in both products.** The Leaderboard's History shows "Edited Alex Johnson: to_par, thru" and "Restored snapshot", and labels every settings save — theme, TV, Calcutta alike — "Updated tournament settings" (UI-LB-16). Calcutta's Undo confirmation names a sale "sell", its Activity table prints "team skip", and its tables render `ACTIVE`, `VOID`, `ON_BLOCK` raw (UI-CA-06, UI-CA-23). Both are the same defect: a database value rendered where a sentence belongs. *Recommendation:* each product gets one label map — `statusLabel()` and an action-name map — keyed by the values the server actually writes, with a test that enumerates the server's own action list and fails on a missing key. *Accept:* no on-screen string in either product contains an underscore or an all-caps enum, other than a deliberate status badge.

**UI-X-05 · P3 · Both · Observed + Source-supported** — **Each product has several names for itself and its own parts, and they are shown in the same room on the same night.** The Leaderboard calls itself "Leaderboard", "TOURNAMENT CENTER", "Tournament center", "TOURNAMENT LEADERBOARD", "Clubhouse leaderboard", "SCORER'S DESK" and "Operator console" (UI-LB-21). Calcutta has a full glossary of drift — buyer/purchaser/party/payee, four names for the TV, four for correcting a sale, Title Case beside sentence case in the same file (UI-CA-27). Because the two products share a clubhouse, an evening and a set of volunteers, the terms they *share* should also agree: **TV display**, **public board**, **flight**, **operator console**, and the same flight names between the leaderboard's flighting and the Calcutta board's pools. *Recommendation:* one short vocabulary list per product, plus a shared list for the five cross-product terms; sentence case everywhere. *Accept:* one name per concept within each product, and identical names for the five shared concepts.

**UI-X-06 · P3 · Both · Source-supported** — **Both TVs are over-configured, and neither is configured from the room.** The Leaderboard offers three independent "after this many pages" integers defaulting to 99, a Top-N field, a flight list, "Also show the whole field", a text size in px ("24–64 px at 1080p"), and two palette controls with different names in different places — with nothing showing the resulting order (UI-LB-15). Calcutta's TV is the best screen in either product but still leaves operator chrome on it in fullscreen (UI-CA-30). The research (§2.3, §2.5) is specific here: the TV is its own surface with *few* settings, and Daktronics' verified rule is about 1 inch of character height per 25 ft of viewing distance (a stricter 1 in/10 ft is also common). *Recommendation:* measure the clubhouse TV's diagonal and viewing distance **once**, set both products' default text size from that number, and replace px fields with Normal / Large / Extra large; the Leaderboard replaces the three page-count integers with one ordered rotation list and a read-only preview line. *Accept:* neither TV settings screen contains a pixel value or a screen resolution; each shows the resulting order or size in words.

**UI-X-07 · P3 · Both · Source-supported** — **Both products say things more than once.** The Leaderboard reports every action twice — a global "Saving… / Saved" banner plus a local note in Calcutta, auto-flighting, quick entry, setup editors and import (UI-LB-06) — states the scoring basis three times above the public table, and offers two skip links to the same target (UI-LB-21). Calcutta says "No money is processed" in at least six places and captions its own "+$25" buttons (UI-CA-26). *Recommendation:* in both, one region owns outcomes and local space shows results; a global rule is stated once, in the footer, and section notices carry only what is specific to that section. *Accept:* one outcome message per action; each standing disclaimer appears once per product.

**UI-X-08 · P3 · Both · Source-supported** — **The same three accessibility defects appear in both, and axe cannot see any of them.** Both products are axe-clean — zero violations across every Leaderboard page and every Calcutta page but one moderate heading rule — yet both (a) drop focus to `<body>` after a destructive or applying action (Leaderboard: Remove, Restore, Apply import — UI-LB-26; Calcutta: Sold and Undo when opened by keyboard — UI-CA-09), (b) attach a live region to a field that echoes the operator's own typing (Leaderboard quick-entry tally per keystroke — UI-LB-11; Calcutta admin `big-bid` per digit — UI-CA-33) while announcing nothing for the outcome that matters, and (c) ship interactive controls under the 24 px floor (Leaderboard 10 px header hints and reorder arrows; Calcutta's 19 px help icon, zero-padding Tools trigger and 25 px roster arrows — UI-CA-31). *Recommendation:* one rule for each in both codebases — never leave `activeElement` on `<body>` after an action; live regions announce outcomes, never input; a measured target-size assertion in each product's browser test. *Accept:* in both apps, `activeElement` is never `<body>` after a destructive action; typing announces nothing; every interactive element measures ≥ 24×24 px.

**UI-X-09 · P3 · Both · Observed** — **Neither product has a removal discipline, and both are accreting fast.** The Leaderboard gained Quick entry, statistics pages, a Calcutta board, themes and auto-flighting inside one week; Calcutta has run UI2, C1, S1, Batch K and Batch L over a comparable span. The research (§2.8) is explicit that the failure mode of this category is breadth — the gala suites — and that the right comparables are the narrow golf tools. Neither product is there yet; the risk is the trajectory. **The two products now also overlap**: the Leaderboard has grown its own Calcutta board while Edwards County Calcutta exists as a separate product on the same VM, which is a product decision worth making deliberately rather than by accretion. *Recommendation:* each batch that adds a control names one it removes or merges; §3.11 and §4.10 are the standing candidate lists. Separately, the owner should decide what the Leaderboard's Calcutta board is *for* relative to the Calcutta product, since both can be shown in the same clubhouse. *Accept:* the next batch in each product closes at least as many controls as it opens; the Calcutta-board overlap has a recorded decision.

**UI-X-10 · P3 · Both · Source-supported** — **Small shared helpers are missing in both, and each is written more than once per product.** The Leaderboard uses four different date/time formats across board, TV, History and Calcutta (UI-LB-23). Calcutta hard-codes `$` in bid entry while offering five currencies, prefixes the ISO code elsewhere, and labels a third field "(currency units)" (UI-CA-19). Both need a status label map (UI-X-04). *Recommendation:* in each product, one date formatter with a date preset and a time preset, one `currencySymbol()`, one `statusLabel()` — three small modules, each used everywhere. *Accept:* grep finds one definition and no ad-hoc formatting at a call site.

### 5.1 What to do with this audit

Nothing here is approved, and nothing in either repository was changed to produce it. The findings are **proposed**, with an evidence class and an acceptance test each so they can be approved individually or in batches.

A reasonable order, by value per unit of risk:

1. **The night-of correctness set, Calcutta** — UI-CA-06, UI-CA-07, UI-CA-08, UI-CA-09, UI-CA-16. Small, local, and each one removes a way to record a wrong number or stall the room. UI-CA-08 in particular can accept a mistyped bid as a real one.
2. **The unconfirmed-destruction set** — UI-CA-15 and UI-LB-09 together, plus UI-X-02's verb labels. One pattern, applied twice.
3. **The compaction set** — UI-X-01: the Leaderboard borrows what Calcutta already ships.
4. **The vocabulary and internals set** — UI-X-04, UI-X-05, UI-CA-27, UI-LB-16. Cheap, highly visible, and it is what a volunteer actually complains about.
5. **Everything in §3.11 and §4.10** — removals, which cost nothing to try and can be reverted in one commit.

### 5.2 What this audit did not establish

Repeating §1's boundary so it is not lost at the bottom of a long document. **Not covered:** the real clubhouse TV, projector and Fire/Smart-TV browsers; any signed-in behaviour against production; physical phones and real screen readers (axe and source review only); Calcutta's Local Users, Access and Activity screens beyond a render; the Leaderboard's statistics pages and its Calcutta-on-TV pages, because the demo event carried no Calcutta plan — the public Calcutta board rendered its "No Calcutta board" empty state, so **UI-LB-20 is source-supported only and has not been seen on screen**.

Every finding marked *Hypothesis* carries the named check that would settle it. No finding in this document was verified against production, and a local pass is not hosted verification.
